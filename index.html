01 / Origin

IT STARTED
WITH A CAT.

This isn’t just a random mascot. PUSHOK has been part of one person’s life for almost 17 years — from a childhood gift to the face of a new internet project.

This little orange cat was a gift from my grandma. I’ve had him for almost 17 years, and he has been with me through pretty much every stage of my life.

One day, while thinking about creating my own crypto project, I looked at him and thought: why not him?

That simple idea became PUSHOK — a project built around a childhood story, internet culture, and a community starting from zero.
